# Shopping Behavior Dashboard

**Tool:** Tableau  · Graduate course: Information Visualization

## The Brief

A shopping-behavior dataset — 3,900 customers tracked over two years — with the goal of designing a dashboard where every chart choice was defensible: the right mark type and visual channel for what each question needed.

## The Dashboard

Six views, each chosen deliberately:
- **Gender Shopping Behaviour** — diverging bar chart by gender/age
- **Performing Categories** — treemap (hierarchical category → sub-category data)
- **Performing States** — geospatial choropleth map
- **Performing Seasons** — line chart (season is inherently ordered)
- **Preferred Size & Color** — bubble chart
- **Review Ratings** — bar chart by category

## Findings

- Male customers substantially outnumber female customers across nearly every age bracket
- Clothing sub-categories dominate purchase volume over accessories, footwear, and outerwear
- Montana and Idaho showed the strongest purchase-amount concentration — not the largest states by population
- Review ratings are stable (~3.0–3.3) across all categories

Tableau · Dashboard design · Visual encoding & idiom selection · Geospatial analysis
